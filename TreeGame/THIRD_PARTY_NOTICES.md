## Sound: Running water

- **File:** `Assets/Audio/Sounds/RunningWater.wav` (originally `sirldrende vann1.wav`; only the file name was changed)
- **Author:** Arthur
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/running-water
- **License used:** CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/. The source also offers GPL 2.0 and GPL 3.0; those aren't used, as they could be read to cover the game's code.
- **Changes:** none. Keep it that way: ShareAlike doesn't reach the game while the sound is included unchanged, but an edited version (trimmed, mixed or effects added) would have to be released under CC BY-SA 3.0 itself.
- **Restriction:** CC BY-SA 3.0 forbids technical measures that restrict the rights it grants, so check before shipping with DRM or on a console.
- **In the game:** credited on the Credits screen; plays when the well puts water in the bucket

## Sound: Grass Foot Step Sounds (Yo Frankie!)

- **Files:** `Assets/Audio/Sounds/FootstepGrassLeft.wav` and `FootstepGrassRight.wav` (originally `sfx_step_grass_l.flac` and `sfx_step_grass_r.flac`)
- **Author:** Blender Foundation, from the game Yo Frankie!; submitted by Lamoot
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/grass-foot-step-sounds-yo-frankie
- **License:** CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- **Changes:** converted from FLAC to 16-bit WAV with VLC, because Unity can't import FLAC. The sound itself is unchanged. The credit says so, as CC BY 3.0 asks for changes to be indicated.
- **Restriction:** CC BY 3.0 forbids technical measures that restrict the rights it grants, so check before shipping with DRM or on a console. It also forbids implying the author endorses the game, so credit the Blender Foundation only as the source of the sound.
- **In the game:** credited on the Credits screen; plays as the player walks
